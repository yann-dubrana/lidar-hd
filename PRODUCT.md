# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People choosing French administrative areas and monitoring long-running IGN
LiDAR HD downloads from a keyboard-driven terminal on their desktop.

## Product Purpose

Make it easy to browse or search for an area, understand its estimated storage
cost, and follow download and optional processing without losing context.

## Brand Personality

Calm, practical, readable. SuperFile is the visual reference, with a roomier
layout rather than its dense file listing.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, crowded controls, ornamental animation, and
progress percentages that imply an accurate time estimate.

## Design Principles

- Keep area selection, estimates, and pipeline status visible together.
- Browse regions and departments without requiring a search first.
- Keep expensive processing opt-in and show costs before starting.
- Separate overall stage progress from current-file byte progress.
- Keep multi-area selections across searches and run their jobs sequentially.
- Make all stages independently selectable, including orthophoto-only export.
- Preserve the command-line workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable spacing, persistent key
hints, and textual state labels so colour is never the only status signal.
Use the requested dark, SuperFile-inspired panels with restrained mint and
lavender accents and no decorative animation.
